31/* Resident debug monitor. Opcodes, error codes, and event reasons follow
32 the monitor protocol design (sections 3, 6, 9). The high nibble of a TYPE
33 encodes direction: 0x0_/0x2_ host-originated, 0x4_ PS1 response, 0x8_ PS1
34 async event. */
