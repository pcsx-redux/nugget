sio1-misc results

SCPH-1001, SCPH-5501 and SCPH-7001 give identical readings on every case (112 of 112 pass on each).

The access width does not matter, only the address. A store drives the bus with the source register shifted left by eight times the low two address bits, and the halfword register at that address with bit 0 cleared latches its own 16 bits of the bus:

store 5Ch gets 5Eh gets
sb/sh/sw at 5Ch bits 15-0 of the source unchanged
sb at 5Dh bits 7-0 of the source as its high byte, 00h as its low byte unchanged
sb/sh at 5Eh unchanged bits 15-0 of the source
sb at 5Fh unchanged bits 7-0 of the source as its high byte, 00h as its low byte

A byte store is never dropped and never masked to its lane, and a 32-bit store at 5Ch does not reach 5Eh. 5Ch stores what it latches byte-swapped, with each byte trimmed to the character length in SIO1 mode bits 2-3 (5 bits: (b & 1Fh) | C0h, 6 bits: (b & 3Fh) | 80h, 7 bits: b & 7Fh, 8 bits: unchanged). 5Eh (baud reload) stores it as is. Changing the character length afterwards does not change the stored value. Mode reads back unchanged.

Reads: lw at 5Ch returns 5Eh in the upper half and 5Ch in the lower; lhu at 5Ch and 5Eh and lbu at 5Ch-5Fh return the matching part of that word.

Raw runs: farm tickets on SCPH-1001 and SCPH-5501 with a probe printing every reading, then this test on all three revisions.
